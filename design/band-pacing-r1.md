# agent-relay design: a night paces its spawns by the session band (r1)

**Status:** design r1, 2026-10-14, written before code, one refuter round to come. It amends relay-r1 §9 (configuration) and §15 (the breaker). It consumes the session plugin's admission verdict and never recomputes it; that plugin's `design/admission-r1.md` §2.4 keeps `-p` sessions in `observe` "until agent-relay checks rows itself". The owner's word (agentik-session `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`): helpers run to 97% of every five-hour window (answer 2); nights and sittings are one kind of session, and whoever starts first takes the room (answer 4); when the week resets first, the window fills by the weekly reset (answer 5), the band's horizon.

## 1. The problem

A night picks rows up to `--parallel` and reads no usage (`PickIn`, `internal/night/night.go:1634`). It meets the account only at a limit, by pausing to the reset (usage-limit-resume r1 §2). On 2026-10-14, night 2026-10-14-2 opened at 06:15 with `--parallel 2` and spawned two rows 30 s apart. Replayed through the plugin's `decide` (`claude_session/admission.py:776`) over its ledger as it stood, the first spawn read `admitted` (peak 90%), the second `not-started` (peak 115%), and each two-minute sample from 06:18 to 06:50 read room 0 (peaks 143% to 217%). At 06:48 the live band read 38% used, 141% projected, room 0 and 13 spawns held this window. The relay asked nothing, and its next row spawns when its dependency is met.

## 2. The decision

1. **Present or absent.** The band is present when `agentik-session` resolves at the night's open (`resolveTool`, `internal/effects/pin.go:472`), outside `PinTools`: a pinned tool that moves unruns every later gate (`internal/gate/gate.go:237`), and a plugin reinstall mid-night must not. The relay config's new `band` key is `pace` (the default) or `off`. Absent or off, nothing is asked and every spawn runs as today: the relay works alone (agentik-commons GUIDE §3).
2. **One ask before every spawn.** In `RunRow`, ahead of the breaker (`internal/night/run.go:188`), for every part and orchestrator sitting: `agentik-session admission admit --session <the part's session id>`, stdin `{"model": <its --model>, "subagentType": "relay-row", "background": true, "fork": false, "tool_use_id": "<night>/<row>/<part>"}`, under the relay's environment with a 10 s timeout. The plugin decides inside its lock and appends its own `verdict` line; the relay writes none of its files.
3. **The verdict, read.** `admitted`, `unread` and `stale` spawn. `not-started`, `deferred`, `wait` and `denied` hold. Anything else (a non-zero exit, a timeout, output not one object, an unknown verdict) is `unchecked` and spawns, as the plugin's Mod passes a failed hook (admission-r1 §3.3). The answer's `mode` is recorded, not obeyed: obeying `observe` would never pace.
4. **The hold.** A held row keeps its slot and stage and asks again every `night.BandPoll`, 60 s through the clock, the plugin's own wake clock (admission-r1 §3.4), so a night never out-polls a sitting for the room. One ask at a time per night: held rows queue in arrival order, only the first asks, and the next asks one poll after the first spawns. A hold never fails a row; it ends as the breaker's wait ends (`run.go:203-215`, relay-r1 §15): `stop` leaves the row `skipped` (`stop: owner-stop`); `kill` or a signal fails it `owner-kill`; a sibling's limit hit fails it `usage-limit`, paused to the wake as usage-limit-resume rules. A hold that reaches `--until` leaves the row `skipped` (`stop: until-reached`) with its tree kept, and the night stops as it does at `--until`. A `denied` hold (the week at its ceiling) sends one notice, quiet hours kept: the owner moves to a second account (answer 2), and the hold ends when the readings show room. The sitting's clock (`run.go:186`) starts after the hold.
5. **With entries 11 and 12.** A hold is not a pause. The five-hour hit still pauses the whole night and a model's own hit only its rows (12). A fallback part (11) asks like any part, since the window is the account's. Neither entry changes.

**Rejected.** Reading `band.json`: the plugin marks it display, "Nothing decides on it" (`admission.py:880-882`). It is rewritten only inside a record or an admit (`:940`), so it ages between them, and its room counts the last helper's family (`:927`). Deciding from it copies a freshness rule and the verdict's order (`:784-809`) into Go: session-layer-r1 §1's frozen layer again, a copy taken once and read later as now. The ask reads the plugin's current code and whole ledger at the spawn. Holding in the loop before staging: a new loop state that misses continuations; the RunRow wait reuses the breaker's endings. Stopping on `denied`: the owner would restart the night after switching accounts. A floor of one running row: the window is shared, and the usage-limit pause stays the backstop.

## 3. The seam and the record

- `internal/effects`: a `Band` effect, `Ask(ctx, session, request)`; `OSBand` runs the resolved file, `FakeBand` scripts answers; `band` is read beside `caffeinate` (`config.go:205`).
- `internal/night/run.go`: the wait at `:188`. `RunOptions` gains `Until` and the night's queue of held rows, beside `spacer` (`:106`).
- The ledger: `night-open` gains `band` (the resolved file, `absent` or `off`). A `band` line, as the `breaker` line, marks a hold's start (`state: held`, `verdict`, `peak`, `text`), at its end (`state: admitted`, `asks`, `wait_s`) and on `unchecked` (`error`); each carries `row` and `part`. A spawn admitted at its first ask writes none. The `spawn` line gains `band: {verdict, peak}` and the `sitting` line `band: {asks, wait_s, verdict}`, so `json-keys.golden` is refrozen.
- `status` (`internal/night/status.go:25`, `:47`): a held row reads `held` (display only), its reason the band's text, its elapsed the hold's. `--json` gives the row `band: {verdict, peak, since, asks}`, and `next` leads with `the band holds <rows> since HH:MM; asks again at HH:MM`. `report` counts the holds and their wait. `doctor` shows the resolved file and its `--version`, `absent` or `off`, and never asks (an ask writes a verdict line).
- relay-r1 §9 names `band`; §15 puts the band's wait before the breaker's. No stop reason or state is added.

## 4. The proof

**P1, the census, 2026-10-14 06:55.** A read-only script replayed every relay `spawn` line since the plugin's ledger began (2026-10-12 10:41) through its `decide`, over the ledger up to that second, with §2.2's request. 36 spawns fell in 10 five-hour windows: 27 read `admitted`, 9 `not-started`. The two windows that reached 100% held five of the nine and no admitted spawn. They are the relay's two window pauses: 2026-10-12 11:12, after a row spawned at 99%, and 2026-10-13 23:26, after a row spawned at 100%, with three rows idle 66 minutes to the wake. No window whose relay spawns all read `admitted` passed 88%. Two windows ended under the ceiling with a `not-started` in them, at 60% and 79%. Re-asked each minute, their holds would have lasted 101 to 117 minutes and 12 minutes. Upper bounds: the replay keeps the relay's own burn. The 06:16 spawn still reads held at 06:55, the window at 50%.

**P2, the cost.** `agentik-session --version` takes 0.05 s; one `decide` over the ledger's 4,083 rows, 0.08 s. A held night asks once a minute.

**P3, the weekly horizon.** Today the week resets at 08:00, the window at 10:30, and the plugin's change makes 08:00 the band's horizon. The relay reads neither reset: its input changes, its code does not.

## 5. The cut into packets

1. **AR76, the ask and the hold.** §2 and §3's effect, wait, config and ledger. Named tests in `internal/night` and `internal/effects`, over `FakeBand` and the step clock: absent or `off` asks nothing; `admitted`, `unread`, `stale` spawn at once; `not-started` twice then `admitted` spawns after two polls, two `band` lines, `asks: 3, wait_s: 120`; of two held rows only the first asks, the second one poll after the first spawns; `stop`, `kill` and `--until` end a hold within one poll as §2.4 says; exit 3, a timeout, bad JSON or an unknown verdict spawn with an `unchecked` line; `denied` holds with one notice; a continuation and an orchestrator sitting ask.
2. **AR77, the read.** §3's `status`, `--json`, `report` and `doctor`, by named tests over a held night's ledger.

## 6. Open risks

- **The projection over-reads.** P1's 60% and 79% windows. Calibration is the plugin's (admission-r1 P1 to P3); the owner's lever is `band: off`.
- **A row is projected as a helper.** The plugin adds a helper's term over its 15-minute seed life (admission-r1 §3.2) for a main thread; this morning's rows ran 8 to 36 minutes. Its ask also reads the default ceiling: its session has not resolved yet. Both are filed with the plugin; the usage-limit pause catches an under-read.
- **No bound but `--until` and `stop`.** A night with no `--until` holds for as long as the window reads full. `Night.tla` does not model the hold; the refuter checks whether its liveness leans on the breaker's ten-minute `Max`.
- **Fail open.** A broken plugin turns pacing off, seen only in `unchecked` lines and doctor.
