## 1. What the source says (quoted)

§2: "A gate's verify line takes its root's gate lock just before its clock starts (`gate.go:926`, after the baseline's anchor) and releases it when the line returns". "The lock is process-wide, one slot per cleaned root". "The merge and the measure keep the root's lock … and never take the gate lock". On `stop` or an account stop "a waiting verify keeps its place and runs under its full limit". "Only the owner's kill … closes the row's kill channel. The wait checks it before trying the lock, then selects on both, so a kill wins over a free lock". "The line never runs: the verify reads `unrun`, last `killed waiting for the gate lock after <n> s`, and the row fails as the kill makes it (`owner-kill` …), its tree and branch kept". §3: "`GateLock(root, holder, killed, waiting)` returns the unlock and the seconds waited only once it holds the lock, so when the lock is held at the try it calls `waiting(behind, since)` before blocking. `held` gains the kill channel, closed once in `kill`; the row's `Gate` func receives it." "`Verify.Killed` (nil for a hand gate: never closed). `relay.Check.Wait`, seconds, `json:"-"` as `Cut` is." "`waiting` writes one `verify-wait` line (`row`, `night`, `behind`: the holder's row); the verify's `gate` line gains `wait_s`, absent at 0."

The code at 7450c05: the verify's clock `gate.go:926`, its anchor `:920` (root lock released on return), the merge's clock `:987` under `RootLock` at `:1584`; `LockRoot`'s map `bound.go:262-286`; `held` `night.go:185`, `kill` `:218`, `launch` `:745`; the gate's call `run.go:495-496`; a killed row's close `night.go:1306-1307`.

## 2. Rulings

Standing rules. The charter is `history/sittings/<the build date>-<n>-ar78.md` in doclint's five-part shape, opened at act 1, closed before the last commit, no home path. Each act's first test is red on the pin; its red line goes in the commit message. `DECISIONS.md` and `json-keys.golden` stay: no `--json` key changes (AR79's).

1. **The lock**, after `RootLock`: `gateLocks sync.Map`, one slot per `filepath.Clean(root)`, process-wide, no flock (§2.1). `GateLock` returns `ErrGateKilled` at once when `killed` is closed; takes a free slot at once; else calls `waiting(holder, since)`, selects on `killed` and the slot, then, held or killed, `waiting("", since)` once (the end AR79 reads). A nil `killed` never closes; a nil `waiting` is not called. Real time, as the line's deadline.
2. **The gate.** The holder is `st.Branch`, the gate lines' `row` (`gate.go:1246`), passed to `verify`. Between `limit` and `start` it calls `night.GateLock(root, holder, v.Killed, v.Waiting)` and unlocks as `RunLogged` returns. `c.Wait` is `int(waited/time.Second)`. On `ErrGateKilled`: exit -1, §2.4's last, `unrun`, no line, no log. A nested, skipped or unresolved verify takes no lock; `mergedVerify` and `Hand` are unchanged (nil `Killed`: waits and runs). `run`'s `line` writes `wait_s` when `Wait > 0`.
3. **No override at the close**: `Decide` reads the unrun verify red; the night fails a killed row `owner-kill` (`night.go:1306-1307`).
4. **The channel.** `held` gains `killCh`, made in `launch`; `kill` closes it once (`sync.Once`; nil left alone) before `killFor`, so a kill after `stopFor` still closes it. `stopFor`, `stopAll` and the context never do.
5. **The seam.** `night.GateWait{Killed, Waiting}`; `RunOptions.Gate` gains `w GateWait`, which `launch` fills (`Waiting` puts `verify-wait` `{row, behind}` on a non-empty `behind`) and `RunRow` passes; `main.go`'s closure copies `v`, setting both.
