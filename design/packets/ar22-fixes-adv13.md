## 2. Rulings

The pin, not one more deny-list entry: where it closes a finding, say so and add no guard. The reader's env is an allowlist the relay builds, never the child's env minus keys. The ledger is never an input to a base, a green or resume's exec (memory or the repo's refs are); a ledger value may only red or refuse. Each act's test reproduces its finding FIRST (red on `main`). Record choices in the charter, at most ONE fork.
