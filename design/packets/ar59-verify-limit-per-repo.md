## 1. What the source says (quoted)

`DECISIONS.md` entry 10: "A repo's relay config may carry its own verify limit; when it is unset, the limit stays 900 s. `relay doctor --verify` measures the repo's verify line and suggests a limit. A verify line cut at its limit reads red with a reason that says the line was cut, never with an empty reason."

`const Timeout = 900 * time.Second` (`effects.go:153`) bounds every verify run: `gate.go:908` and `:964`, `plan.go:849`, `verify.go:93`. The shell returns exit 124 at the deadline (`shell.go:223`); `relay.Decide` returns `RedResult, ""` for a red verify (`internal/relay/gate.go:161`).

## 2. Rulings

1. **Act 1, the key.** `verify_seconds` (named as the queue's `measure_seconds`), read in any layer as every key is and documented under the repo's `agent-relay` section. Unset: 900 s (`effects.Timeout`, unchanged). An integer from 1 to 3600; anything else (`0`, `-1`, `3601`, `15m`) is a `LoadConfig` error naming the line and the key. The bound: a green row runs the verify line twice (gate, then merge) and the merge holds the root's lock through it (§6), so 3,600 s holds a row's close to two hours and the root to one.
2. **Act 2, where it applies.** The gate's verify, the merge's (`MergeIfGreen` reads `Gate.VerifySeconds`, set by the gate) and the plan's verify baseline (the measure's budget still caps it). `relay plan`, `run` and `gate` pass `cfg.VerifyLimit()`. Acceptance lines keep 900 s. `Hand`'s `source string` becomes `v Verify`, of which it reads `Source` and `Limit`.
3. **Act 3, a cut says so.** A verify line is cut when `effects.Cut(exit, took, limit)`: exit 124 and its real time (`time.Since`, never `fx.Clock`: the deadline is real time) at or past the limit; a line that exits 124 sooner is a plain red. A cut check is red with `Cut` the limit in whole seconds, and its `gate` line carries `cut`. `Decide` returns `red, verify-cut` for a gate whose verify was cut, after every named reason and ahead of every red with an empty one. The end line reads `"reason":"verify-cut"`, `"detail":"verify cut at <n> s"`; the night's row takes `verify-cut` as its reason (`run.go`). A cut merge verify refuses with `merge <b>: verify cut at <n> s, last "<last>"`, recorded `aborted`. `VerifyCut` is a packet failure, absent from `StopReasons`.
4. **Act 4, doctor suggests.** `relay doctor --verify` runs the line under 3,600 s, not the repo's limit, so a line past its limit is still measured. It prints `limit: 900 s (unset)` or `limit: 1800 s (verify_seconds)`. For a line that exits 0 it suggests twice the measured time, rounded up to a whole minute, at least 900 and at most 3600 (13 minutes reads 1560), and prints `suggest: verify_seconds: 1560 in .claude/claude-code-session/config.yaml, section agent-relay`. A red or cut line suggests nothing and says why. `--json` gains `limit` and `suggest` (0: none); exit codes are unchanged.
5. **Tests.** effects: `_1_VerifySeconds` (1800, unset 900, ruling 1's refusals, 3600 taken, not `Unknown`); `_3_Cut` (`Cut(124, l, l)` true, `Cut(124, l-1ms, l)` and `Cut(1, l, l)` false); `_4_Suggest` (13m 1560, 100s 900, 7m30.2s 960, 31m 3600). relay: `_3_DecideVerifyCut` (cut reads `verify-cut`, with a red acceptance line too; uncut red reads `""`; `main-moved` kept). gate: `_2_GateUsesLimit` (`Verify{Limit: 1s}` over `sleep 3`: red, cut 1; no limit: green); `_2_HandTakesLimit` (`red, verify-cut`); `_3_CutReason` (the ledger's `cut` 1, `verify-cut`, `verify cut at 1 s`; `exit 124` under 10 s: red, empty reason, no `cut`); `_3_MergeNamesCut` (a green gate built by hand, `VerifySeconds: 1`, `sleep 3`: the error and the `merge` line name the cut, the root unmoved). night: `_2_PlanPassesLimit` (a shell recording its timeout, `VerifyLimit` 1200 s, `MeasureSeconds` 3600, a still clock: verify gets 1200 s, an acceptance line 900 s); `_3_RowReason` (a gate returning `red, verify-cut`: the row's reason). cmd/relay: `_4_DoctorSuggests` (`TestAR25_3_DoctorVerify`'s fixture: `limit` 900, `suggest` 900; with `verify_seconds: 1800` in its repo config, `limit` 1800; plain output carries `suggest: verify_seconds: 900`); `_4_Gofmt` (`go/format.Source` of each `.go` in `copyIn(t, "../..", "cmd", "internal")`, testdata skipped, equals the file, naming each that differs; `gofmt` is off the allowlist).
