0. **The gate binds a sha (ADV6-3, -4, -10).** `gather` reads `Git.Branch(st.Path)` first: not `st.Branch` is `red`; then `g.Head`, and every later fact reads that sha. `DiffNames` runs `--no-renames` (`report.go` too). `MergeIfGreen` merges `g.Head`, refusing a moved tip (`merge` line `moved`). `relay gate` and `loadPlan` load from `Git.Root`, never the cwd's toplevel; the verify line is resolved once on main, never in the worktree; a root without `check:` is `skipped`, a failed probe `unrun`. `TestAR13_0_GateBindsBranchSha`, `TestAR13_0_VerifyResolvedOnMain`. Model: `MergedShaIsGated`, in `Night.cfg`.
1. **A red main never merges (ADV6-5; R6 §4).** The merge's verify passes green only. `Plan.Verify` red: `Print` says `main is red: merge-on-green rows land branch-only`, the `merge` line notes `main red`. `as main` acceptance lines stay; `relay.Check` gains `Head`, compared only at the head it was measured at; a moved main reads the memo there, a miss is `unrun: stale-baseline`. `TestAR13_1_RedMainNeverMerges`, `TestAR13_1_AnchorCarriesHead`. Model: `Merge(r)` takes `green` alone.
2. **The dead relay (ADV6-2).** `night.Run` traps INT, TERM, HUP once: the night stops `owner-kill`, `detail: signal:<name>`; every live child group and line group gets TERM, `KillGrace`, KILL; the sitting lines are written; exit 1. `<state>/nights/<night>/groups.json` holds `{pgid, started, row}` per spawn and line, cleared at exit; `Run` first reads every night's file, kills a group whose start time still matches (a `reaped` line), drops the rest. `TestAR13_2_SignalReapsGroups`, `TestAR13_2_NextStartReaps`, `TestAR13_2_ReusedPidNotKilled`.
3. **The held stream, the escaped descendant, the second stall (ADV6-7, -8; R6 §2).** A pipe still held past `PipeGrace` after the exit, the sweep and (in `RunRow`) a kill plus `KillGrace` is closed from the read end and counted: a line's is its error (unrun); a row's is a survivor: `failed` `orphans`, `stopped:orphans`; `RunRow` returns within `KillGrace + PipeGrace + 1 s` of a kill. Each watchdog poll snapshots the child's descendants by ppid; the sweep kills the survivors. A second `stalled` row in one night stops it: `relay.StalledTwice`. With ar12 merged, `TestE2E_6_Hold` expects `orphans` within 30 s; `make e2e` once at close. `TestAR13_3_HeldStreamIsOrphans`, `TestAR13_3_ShellHeldPipeUnrun`, `TestAR13_3_DescendantsSwept`, `TestAR13_3_SecondStallStopsNight`. Model: `NoOrphanAfterExit` covers `holdsStdout`.
4. **The memo and the locks (ADV6-9; R6 §4; §16 gap 3).** `LockRoot` adds `flock` on `<state>/locks/root-<sha1(root)>` under the mutex, across each line and the merge. `Memo.Save`: `os.CreateTemp`, the file re-read under the lock and merged by root before the rename. `boundShell` re-reads HEAD and `git status` after a line and stores only when both are unchanged; `ts` over `MemoMaxAge` (24 h) is a miss. The stage lock holds `pid started worktree`; a dead or restarted pid is reclaimed (`lock-reclaimed` line); `relay clean --stale` removes such locks and worktrees whose tip equals its cut. `TestAR13_4_RootLockAcrossProcesses`, `TestAR13_4_MemoStoresOnlyUnmovedHead`, `TestAR13_4_MemoSaveKeepsBothWriters`, `TestAR13_4_MemoAgeIsAMiss`, `TestAR13_4_StaleLockReclaimed`, `TestAR13_4_BudgetBeforeProbe`.
5. **The breaker's clock, the burst, the probe (R6 §4; ADV6-13).** `Breaker.Wait` takes the night's context: a `stop` or `kill` during a wait ends it within one poll (`skipped`, or `failed owner-kill`). `--parallel` over `runtime.NumCPU()` is usage, exit 2.
